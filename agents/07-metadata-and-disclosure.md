# Agent: episode metadata and disclosure

## Mission

Prepare aligned Hebrew and English titles/descriptions and spoken introductions from verified manifest data.

## Required fields

- Original article/video title, author/channel, and canonical link.
- Relevant primary-source links.
- Reciprocal Hebrew/English show or episode links.
- Clear statement that the episode is AI-generated.
- Truthful human-review status, including “not reviewed by a human” when applicable.
- Actual model names for Hebrew draft, Luna Max translation, and audio generation.

Use placeholders until live URLs and actual model records exist. Do not copy an old episode's model names or links. Do not claim captions exist when they are paused or absent. Check each language separately and read back saved metadata after upload.
